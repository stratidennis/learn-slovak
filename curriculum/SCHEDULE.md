# Schedule — the first 16 weeks, day by day

45 minutes a day, six days a week; Sunday is light. The app enforces none of this — it is the
default plan the scheduler proposes, and it bends to your actual review load. Times are the
§9.2 template: **3 drill · 12 SRS · 10 new · 10 input · 7 output · 3 progress**.

## Every day, regardless

- **SRS first** if you only have 12 minutes. The scheduler protects retention; new material can
  wait a day, reviews cannot.
- New-card cap starts at 12/day; the app lowers it automatically if reviews exceed 15 minutes
  three days running.
- The 3-minute drill always targets your worst A3 contrast *or* your top mistake-diary tag —
  whichever is worse this week.

## What the app tracks to adapt this

| Signal | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Reviews > 15 min for 3 days | new-card cap −3 |
| A3 contrast < 80% | that contrast becomes the daily drill until 3 clean days |
| Same mistake tag 5× in a week | a 2-minute targeted drill is inserted before new material |
| Can-do list for the unit fully ticked | unit's roleplay unlocks; next unit starts tomorrow |
| Coverage meter stalls for 2 weeks | suggests a domain pack (Phase 3) early |

## Phase 3 onward (months 4–8)

Domain packs are self-paced: pick one, 2 weeks each, same daily template, Saturday roleplay
from the pack. Phase 4 replaces the "new material" block with 20 minutes in the i+1 reader.

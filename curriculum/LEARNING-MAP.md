# Learning map

The whole course on one page. Phases come from research §9; the units inside each phase come
from the official *Studia Academica Slovaca* standards (*Témy a ciele A1/A2*, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 —
`pipeline/work/reference/`), re-ordered by **communicative need first, frequency second**
(§15.3). Grammar is listed where it is first *used*; it is explained on tap, never as a chapter.

Two rules from the research drive the shape:

1. **Frequency decides *what*; need decides *when*** — band-1 words are trusted, everything else
   is hand-placed into the unit where you actually need it.
2. **Chunks before grammar** — every unit opens with 8–12 chunks you can use the same day.

Estimated pace assumes the §9.2 session (~45 min/day). Unit length is deliberately uneven: a
unit ends when its can-do statements are ticked, not after N lessons.

## Phase 0 — Sounds & script *(days 1–5)*

| Unit | You will be able to… | Content | Exercises |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0.1 The alphabet Romanians already know** | read any Slovak word aloud; spell your own name | **Alphabet screen**: 46 letters, each with its Slovak *name* (á, bé, cé… for spelling), IPA, Romanian anchor, example word, audio for both; §4.1 sound map; `ch dz dž` as single letters; the *de te ne le* softening rule. Every Slovak string in the app carries a **Romanian respelling** (PROsiim si CAAvu — CAPS = stress, doubled vowel = long, ɦ = voiced h) and IPA, toggled in Settings (D121) | E4 internationalisms on sight; D1 chunk shadow |
| **0.2 The four things Romanian ears miss** | hear length, ť/ď/ň/ľ, h vs ch, first-syllable stress | Appendix B minimal pairs, hunspell-verified; Piper + Commons human audio | A3 minimal pairs (the daily micro-drill is born here) |
| **0.3 Words you already own** | recognise 84 cognates and 16 false friends | §4.3, §4.4 as cards | E2 cognate deck, E3 false friends |
| **0.4 Type it** | type every diacritic without looking | keyboard helper; dead-key hints (macOS/Windows/phone) | dictation of single words |
| **0.5 First twenty chunks** | say hello, thanks, sorry, yes, no, I don't understand | Appendix C first 20 | D1 shadow ×20, A1 dictation |

*Exit:* A3 accuracy ≥ 85% on every contrast; can type *ľščťžýáíéďňôäú* unaided.

## Phase 1 — Survival *(weeks 1–6, ≈ A1)* — band 1, ~150 chunks

Mapped to SAS A1 thematic areas 1–8, but ordered by what you need in your first weeks in a
Slovak-speaking setting: greet → get coffee → buy food → move around → talk about yourself.

| Unit | SAS area | You will be able to… | Chunks (examples) | Grammar *used* (note unlocked) | Domain seed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1.1 Hello, sorry, thanks** | 1 Pozdravy | greet formally/informally, thank, apologise, say goodbye; tell *ty* from *vy* | *Dobrý deň / Dobrý* · *Ahoj / Čau* · *Ďakujem (pekne) / Vďaka* · *Prosím* · *Prepáčte / Prepáč* · *Nech sa páči* · *Dovidenia / Dovi* · *Maj sa / Majte sa* | *byť* present; *vykanie* vs *tykanie* | — |
| **1.2 I don't understand** | 1 | ask for repetition, slower speech, meaning; say what you speak | *Nerozumiem* · *Ešte raz, prosím* · *Pomalšie, prosím* · *Ako sa povie … po slovensky?* · *Čo znamená …?* · *Hovorím po anglicky / po rumunsky* · *Učím sa po slovensky* · *Neviem* | *ne-* negation; *po + -sky* | — |
| **1.3 Who I am** | 1, 8 | introduce yourself: name, origin, job, where you live, languages | *Volám sa …* · *Som z Rumunska* · *Som Rumun / Rumunka* · *Som programátor* · *Bývam v Bratislave* · *Pracujem ako …* · *Teší ma* | present tense *-ám/-ím/-em* 1sg/2sg; gender suffix *-ka*; *v* + city | work/tech: *programátor, firma, softvér* |
| **1.4 Coffee** | 5 Jedlo | order a drink, ask the price, pay | *Prosím si kávu* · *Dám si …* · *Koľko to stojí?* · *Platím* · *Účet, prosím* · *Tu alebo so sebou?* · *Dobrú chuť* · *Na zdravie* | **accusative** singular (*kávu, pivo, čaj*); *jeden/jedna/jedno*; *euro/eurá/eur* | food/market |
| **1.5 Numbers, prices, time** | 1, 3 | count to 100; understand prices and clock times spoken fast | *Koľko je hodín?* · *Je pol tretej* · *O koľkej?* · *dve eurá päťdesiat* · *dvadsať deka* | numbers 0–100; 2–4 vs 5+ agreement | market |
| **1.6 The market** | 5 | buy food by weight, ask for things, say that's all | *Prosím si pol kila jabĺk* · *Máte …?* · *Dáte mi …* · *Ešte niečo? — To je všetko* · *Nemáte drobné?* · *Za koľko?* | genitive after quantities (*kila jabĺk*) as chunks; *mať rád* | **food/market pack starts** |
| **1.7 Where is…?** | 6 Mestá | ask and understand simple directions; name places | *Kde je …?* · *Kde sú toalety?* · *Rovno, doľava, doprava* · *Je to ďaleko?* · *Idem do …* · *Som v …* | **locative** *v/na* as chunks (*v Bratislave, na trhu, v práci*); *do* + genitive as chunk | transport |
| **1.8 My day** | 3 Denný program | say what you usually do; days, parts of day | *Ráno vstávam o siedmej* · *Pracujem z domu* · *Cez víkend …* · *Rád čítam / Rada čítam* | present tense all persons; *rád/rada* + verb; *v pondelok, cez víkend* | — |
| **1.9 Likes, feelings, small talk** | 3, 8 | say what you like, how you feel, react | *Páči sa mi to* · *Chutí mi* · *Mám rád/rada …* · *To je super / fajn / v pohode* · *Fakt?* · *Jasné* · *Bolí ma hlava* · *Je mi zle* | **dative experiencer** (*páči sa mi* ≈ *îmi place*); *to je* + neuter adj | — |
| **1.10 Phone & messages** | 2, 7 | answer the phone, send a short message, say you're late | *Haló?* · *Tu je …* · *Zavolám ti* · *Napíš mi* · *Som na ceste* · *Meškám* · *Prepáčte, že meškám* | future *budem* + inf (recognition); imperative *napíš/zavolaj* as chunks | work/tech |
| **1.11 Home & things** | 4 Priestor | name rooms and objects, say where things are, report a problem | *Kde je nabíjačka?* · *Nefunguje to* · *Je to pokazené* · *Aké je heslo na wifi?* · *Hore / dole / vpravo / vľavo* | nominative plural (*dvere, okuliare*); *dva/dve/tri/štyri* + noun | work/tech |
| **1.12 Family & people** | 8 Môj život | talk about family, describe people simply | *Mám brata a sestru* · *Moja žena / môj muž* · *Má modré oči* · *Je milý* | possessives *môj/moja/moje*; animate masc. plural (*bratia*) as recognition | — |
| **1.13 Sunday** ✝ | — (own) | greet and respond at church; the Lord's Prayer as a fixed text | *Pán Boh zaplať* · *Pokoj vám* · *Otče náš …* · *Amen* · *bohoslužba / omša / služby Božie* | fixed text — no analysis | **church pack starts** |

*Exit milestones (§9 Phase 1):* a 2-minute self-introduction; a market purchase roleplay (R1);
understand slow "where / when / how much" questions. Coverage meter ≈ 72%.

## Phase 2 — The sentence engine *(weeks 7–16, ≈ A2)* — band 2, aspect pairs, the big four cases

Mapped to SAS A2 areas. Here grammar becomes systematic *after* six weeks of using it in chunks.

| Unit | SAS A2 area | You will be able to… | Grammar (systematic) | Aspect pairs introduced |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2.1 Yesterday** | 3 Práca a kariéra | narrate what you did | **past tense** (*bol som, robil/robila som*; *išiel/išla*); clitic order *som ho videl* | *robiť/urobiť, ísť/prísť/odísť* |
| **2.2 Tomorrow** | 3 | make plans, appointments | **future** *budem* + inf; perfective present = future (*urobím, stretneme sa*) | *písať/napísať, kupovať/kúpiť* |
| **2.3 Whose, of what** | 4 Obchody | quantities, possession, from/to | **genitive** systematic: *z/od/do/bez/u/okolo*; after numbers 5+; *veľa/málo* | *dávať/dať* |
| **2.4 Where vs. where to** | 1 Priestor, 5 Cestovanie | location vs motion | **accusative vs locative** with *na*, *do/v*, *pod/nad/pred/za/medzi* | *chodiť/ísť* |
| **2.5 To whom** | 2 Život online | give, send, call, write to people | **dative**: *volať, poslať, kúpiť niekomu niečo*; *je mi zima* | *hovoriť/povedať* |
| **2.6 With what, with whom** | 5 | transport, company | **instrumental**: *autom, električkou, s kamarátom* | *cestovať* |
| **2.7 Shopping properly** | 4 | clothes, sizes, returns, online orders | conditional *chcel by som*; comparison *lepší, väčší, viac* | *skúsiť/vyskúšať, platiť/zaplatiť* |
| **2.8 Work & tech** | 2, 3 | talk about your job, tools, a bug, a meeting | imperative systematic; *ktorý* relative clauses | *nasadiť* (deploy), *opraviť*, *stiahnuť* — and the real-life Anglicisms (*deploynuť, pushnúť*) tagged colloquial |
| **2.9 Health & pharmacy** | 8 Zdravie | describe symptoms, understand instructions | *bolí ma*; imperatives at the doctor (*sadnite si, dýchajte*) | — |
| **2.10 Slovakia & Romania** | 6 Slovensko a moja krajina | compare, explain where you're from | adjectives from countries; *ako* comparisons | — |
| **2.11 Family & relationships** | 7 Rodina | describe people, relationships, invite, congratulate | animate masculine forms; *dvaja/traja* | — |
| **2.12 After church** ✝ | — | small talk after the service; the shared core church vocabulary (§9.1) | reflexive *modliť sa*; fixed texts | church pack |

*Exit milestones:* tell a 5-sentence story about your weekend; 3-minute conversation on daily
topics (R2–R4); write a 60-word message. Coverage ≈ 84%.

## Daily session (§9.2, 45 min) — what the app schedules

| Min | Block | Draws from |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Pronunciation micro-drill on your weakest contrast | A3 |
| 12 | SRS reviews | E1 (A1 / C1 / B2 rotation) |
| 10 | New material: 8–12 sentences, ≤ 6 new lemmas | current unit; A2 → A1 → D1 |
| 10 | Input: one podcast/text at your level, unknown ≤ 5% | B4 / B5 / X3 / X6 |
| 7 | Output: 5 RO→SK prompts **or** a roleplay (R-prompts) **or** a 60-word message | C1 / D3 / C5 |
| 3 | Progress: coverage %, can-do tick, next grammar note | G1–G3 |

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Extra |
|---|---|
| Mon–Fri | the 45-minute session |
| Wed | one **creative** exercise (X-list) instead of the input block |
| Sat | **roleplay day**: one R-prompt with an AI tutor, 10 min; paste the transcript into the mistake diary |
| Sun | verse of the day (X1) + church chunks; no SRS pressure — reviews just carry over |
| every 2nd week | G4 export → reviewer / R3 feedback prompt; C5 writing task |

## Milestone gates (how you know you can move on)

| From → to | Gate |
|---|---|
| 0 → 1 | A3 ≥ 85% per contrast; 20 chunks shadowed; can type all diacritics |
| 1 → 2 | 120 chunks "known"; coverage ≥ 70%; R1 (market) roleplay completed without switching to English; SAS A1 can-do list ≥ 80% ticked |
| 2 → 3 | all six cases pass F2 case sorter ≥ 80%; 60 aspect pairs known; R2–R4 done; 60-word message reviewed OK |
| 3 → 4 | one full podcast episode at ≥ 95% understood without transcript; 10-minute conversation |

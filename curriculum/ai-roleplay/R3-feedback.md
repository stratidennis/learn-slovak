# R3 — Feedback on something I wrote or said (no roleplay)

Use this for the 60-word writing task, a voice-note transcript, or a roleplay transcript from
another session.

```
I am a Romanian learning Slovak at level {{LEVEL}}. Below is something I wrote (or a transcript
of something I said). Review it as a friendly native Slovak speaker who is also a good teacher.

RULES
- Do not rewrite the whole text. Point at specific things.
- Distinguish three kinds of issue and label each one:
  [WRONG]  — a real error (case ending, gender, aspect, word order, wrong word)
  [ODD]    — grammatical but not what a native would say; give the natural version
  [STYLE]  — fine, but there is a more colloquial / more natural spoken alternative
- For each [WRONG], give a ONE-LINE explanation in English, and where a Romanian analogy helps
  (e.g. "like îmi place — dative"), use it.
- Pay extra attention to: {{WEAK_TAGS}}.
- Then give a corrected version of the text that stays as close as possible to my wording.
- End with three sentences from my text (corrected) that are worth memorising, each with its
  Romanian meaning.
- Keep the whole review under 300 words. Be direct; skip praise unless it is specific.

MY TEXT:
"""
{{TEXT}}
"""
```
